Identical prices and differences within published rounding precision count as reconciled. Component matching requires compatible charge type, export charge/reward direction, time band, season and physical quantity; kWh and kVAh, and kW and kVA, remain distinct except where the distributor document does not establish the demand quantity (flagged unit unverified).

A difference counts as explained only by a documented adjustment (`scripts/adjustments.py`, shared with the tariff database's `price_adjustment` table), and only when the tariff is inside the scope the sources establish and the amount read from a source reproduces the difference within the published rounding of both values. Anything else stays `unexplained`; a uniform offset alone is never promoted to an explanation.

- **`metering_adder` ({metering} components).** The AER prints network prices without metering and publishes metering separately (2025-26 sheet text: "Metering prices can be found on the metering worksheet"); the distributors fold it into the daily charge. Distributor c/day = AER c/day + 100 x metering $/yr / 365, on fixed charges only.
  - Endeavour Energy 2024-25 to 2026-27: the small-customer tariffs the AER Metering sheet lists beside the metering price (N50, N54, N62, N63, N70-N73, N90-N93, N95-N97); not N19, N20, N29, N39 or N89. Amount: AER Metering sheet (2025-26 on) or "Tariff schedule 1" of the 2024-25 stakeholder report.
  - Essential Energy 2024-25 to 2026-27: LV tariffs (BLN*) except controlled load BLNC1AU/BLNC2AU and unmetered BLNP1AO; HV and sub-transmission tariffs carry none. Essential's price list: "Please note this metering cost is included in the relevant LV tariffs daily charge, however the published AER standard control services pricing model lists the metering charge separately on the Tariff Schedule tab."
  - Energex and Ergon Energy 2025-26 and 2026-27 (none in 2024-25): the amount is the per-tariff Metering block ($/day) printed next to the DUoS, TUoS and JS blocks in the distributor's Schedule 8 workbook (NUoS = DUoS + TUoS + JS + Metering); tariffs without a Metering block value (CAC and large tariffs) carry none.
- **`lfit_adder` ({lfit} components).** Evoenergy's LFiT-inclusive schedules add a single stated amount to every consumption charge (c/kWh); fixed and demand charges are unchanged: +0.258 c/kWh in 2024-25 (schedule of charges, p3), +1.593 in 2025-26 and +3.035 in 2026-27 ("Network tariffs" sheet, cell B6), e.g. "equivalent to an additional 1.593 cents per kilowatt-hour (kWh). The LFiT cost has been applied uniformly to the consumption charges (c/kWh) in Evoenergy's tariffs." It applies only where the distributor side is that LFiT-inclusive document (the 2026-27 comparison uses Evoenergy's schedule without LFiT, which equals the AER).
- **Unexplained ({unexplained} components)**: Evoenergy 2023-24. Evoenergy's statement gives the LFiT rebate as "a reduction of 2.27 cents per kilowatt-hour (kWh) ... on average, across Evoenergy's tariffs", which does not fix the amount for any one component.
