Across the 14 distributors and four years, every value difference between the AER file and the distributor's own publication falls into one of these documented classes (counts are in the per-cell detail, section 8, and in `discrepancies.csv` column `explanation`):

- **Metering embedded in the distributor's fixed charge (`metering_in_fixed`)** - Energex 2025-26/2026-27 (+12.1 / +12.3 c/day on every fixed charge), Ergon Energy 2025-26/2026-27 (+11.5 / +11.6 c/day), Endeavour Energy 2024-25/2025-26/2026-27 (+3.40 / +3.47 / +3.64 c/day on every Daily Access Charge except N19), Essential Energy 2024-25 to 2026-27 (+16.6 / +17.0 / +17.4 c/day on every Network Access charge). The AER files keep metering on a separate worksheet ("Metering prices can be found on the metering worksheet", 2025-26 sheet header) whereas these distributors publish a NUoS fixed charge that includes the legacy metering charge (Endeavour Table 1a note: "Network prices comprise Distribution (DUOS) charges including Metering, Transmission (TUOS) passthroughs and recovery of the NSW Climate Change Fund (CCF) and Energy Infrastructure Roadmap (EIR) contributions"; Essential 2025-26/2026-27 footnote on the LV access charges: "From 1 July 2024 legacy metering costs were reclassified as standard control services and are now recovered in network charges... this metering cost is included in the relevant LV tariffs daily charge, however the published AER standard control services pricing model lists the metering charge separately"; Energex/Ergon price lists show NUoS = DUoS + TUoS + JS + Metering). Energy and demand rates in those cells are identical.
- **ACT Large-scale Feed-in Tariff (`jurisdictional_scheme`)** - Evoenergy 2023-24, 2024-25 and 2025-26. Evoenergy's published schedules are "LFiT adjusted" / "incl LFiT" (a uniform adder on every c/kWh consumption rate: +0.258 c/kWh in 2024-25, +1.593 c/kWh in 2025-26, +3.035 c/kWh in the 2026-27 "incl LFiT" workbook) while the AER stakeholder/consolidated reports exclude Evoenergy LFiT costs (stated on the 2025-26 and 2026-27 sheets). In 2023-24 the sign is reversed: Evoenergy's Statement of Tariff Classes applies the LFiT **rebate** inside its JS column (p21; -2.270 c/kWh on most energy rates, allocated to demand/capacity charges on demand tariffs), while the proposal lodged with the AER is excl LFiT. For 2026-27 Evoenergy also publishes an "AER-approved (excl LFiT)" workbook, and that one matches the AER file exactly (100% exact), which confirms the explanation.
- **Rounding (`equal_after_rounding`)** - the AER files hold unrounded model outputs (e.g. Essential "406.1573769016471 $/year", Power and Water "0.0255972821 $/kWh", AusNet 2025-26 "145.30522170722094") or coarser roundings (SA Power Networks ZSS 0.0272 vs published 0.0273 $/kWh); distributors publish 2 to 6 decimals. All such pairs agree within half a unit of the coarser publication.
- **Units and periods** - the AER 2024-25 and 2025-26 files write demand units without a billing period ("$/kVA", "$dollars/kW") and label the measurement window instead ("Ann Dmnd Pk", "Mth Dmnd"); SA Power Networks bills annual-demand tariffs per day ($/kVA/day), Jemena per year ($/kVA pa), AusNet and Essential per month. Values agree once the distributor's stated period is used; the reconciler reports these as equal with unit_std showing the soft period (e.g. `c/kVA/year?` vs `c/kVA/day`).
- **GST** - no GST differences were found: every AER file states "ALL PRICES ARE EXCLUDING GST" on its Tariff schedule sheet and every distributor document either states GST-exclusive prices or prints both tables (Ausgrid p2/p4, Endeavour Tables 1b/3b, Essential 2024-25, Power and Water 2023-24), which the parsers drop or cross-check.
- **Component basis** - no basis differences: every cell is compared on NUoS (total network price). Where a distributor publishes the split (Energex, Ergon, SAPN, CitiPower/Powercor/UE, Jemena, AusNet, TasNetworks, Evoenergy 2023-24, Ausgrid/Endeavour 2023-24 proposals) NUoS = DUoS + TUoS/DPPC + JSA was verified by the parsers (documented in their summaries; worst deviation 0.0042 c/kWh from a 2 dp NUoS rounding in AusNet 2026-27).
- **Mid-year variations** - none detected: no cell shows a uniform re-pricing offset that is not already explained by metering or LFiT. The AER 2026-27 changelog items (Ausgrid EA335 off-peak charging component, AusNet export reward sign, AusNet public-lighting codes) are in the final v5 file used here.
