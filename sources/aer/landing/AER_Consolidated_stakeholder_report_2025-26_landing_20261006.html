AER - Consolidated stakeholder report 2025–26

Breadcrumb

  1. Home
  2. AER - Consolidated stakeholder report 2025–26

We have consolidated all proposed and approved 2025–26 electricity prices into one file for stakeholders.

This file also contains cost movement analysis for the main flat-rate or block tariffs.

Tariffs in this document:

  • Are approved for ACT, NSW, NT, QLD, SA, TAS, and VIC.
    • For Ausgrid, Energex, Essential Energy, Jemena, Power and Water Corporation, and TasNetworks this includes changes from originally proposed tariffs.
  • Do not include Evoenergy's application of costs related to the ACT large-scale feed-in tariff scheme.

For more information, see the relevant pricing proposals.

Changelog
  • On 8 April we published version 1 with:
    • Proposed prices for ACT, NSW, NT, TAS, and VIC.
    • Price movement analysis.
  • On 10 April we published version 2 with:
    • Updated price movement analysis for AusNet Services and United Energy to correct errors.
  • On 14 May we published version 3 with:
    • Approved prices for ACT, NSW, TAS, and VIC (including updated prices for Essential Energy, Jemena, and TasNetworks, and a single additional price for Ausgrid).
    • Added proposed prices for QLD and SA.
    • Retained proposed prices for NT.
    • Updated price movement analysis.
  • On 16 May we published version 4 with:
    • Approved prices for NT (including updated prices).
    • Updated price movement analysis for NT.
  • On 26 May we published version 5 with:
    • Approved prices for QLD and SA (including updated prices for Energex public lighting).
    • Tariff codes for standard network tariffs.

On 9 May 2025, we approved United Energy's annual pricing proposal for the period 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026. This follows an assessment to ensure consistency with the National Electricity Rules and the 2021–26 revenue determination.

Provided below is our: 

  • statement of reasons providing a summary of our decision, average impacts for residential and small business customers on the most common tariffs, and commentary on drivers of movements
  • a consolidated stakeholder report with proposed/approved network prices for all networks, as well as cost movement analysis.
